# Bank Customer, Loan & Credit Card Dashboard

## 📌 The Problem

A bank's leadership needs a fast, reliable way to answer basic portfolio-health questions — how many customers are actually active, how much loan exposure is outstanding, which accounts are turning into NPAs (non-performing assets), and how much credit risk sits in the card portfolio. Without a consolidated view, this data is scattered across separate customer, loan, and credit card records, making it slow and error-prone to spot risk early.

## 🎯 The Solution

Built an Excel-based analytics dashboard that consolidates customer, branch, loan, and credit card data into a single management view — turning raw multi-table banking data into a KPI dashboard that surfaces portfolio size, customer engagement, and credit risk at a glance.

## 📊 Key Metrics (from the dashboard)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | 1,050 |
| Active Customers | 866 (82%) |
| Total Branches | 30 |
| Loans Disbursed | ₹1.147M |
| Total Loans Outstanding | ₹364M |
| NPA / Default Loans | 92 |
| Total Credit Cards | 500 |
| Credit Card Outstanding | ₹94M |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **82% customer activity rate** (866 of 1,050) indicates healthy overall engagement across the branch network.
- **Loan disbursement (₹1.147M) vs. outstanding (₹364M)** highlights the gap between what's been lent and what remains on the books — a key input for repayment and portfolio-movement analysis.
- **92 NPA/default accounts** were isolated as the specific segment requiring credit-risk attention, rather than leaving risk buried in the full loan book.
- **₹94M in credit card outstanding** adds a second, separate view of customer credit exposure alongside the loan portfolio.

## ⚠️ Risk Considerations & Next Steps

- NPA/default accounts should be monitored on an ongoing basis to limit potential credit losses.
- Loan disbursement vs. outstanding trends should be reviewed further to understand repayment behavior over time.
- Customer activity could be segmented by branch and product to identify specific underperforming areas rather than a single aggregate rate.
- This dashboard provides descriptive, point-in-time insights; a deeper risk model would require transaction-level and repayment-history data.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

Microsoft Excel · PivotTables · Excel Charts · Data Cleaning · Dashboarding · KPI Analysis

## 📁 Outcome

A single, consolidated management dashboard that replaces scattered customer/loan/card records with one KPI view — enabling faster identification of risk accounts and portfolio trends than reviewing raw tables directly.
