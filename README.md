# Bank Customer, Loan & Credit Card Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
An Excel-based banking analytics dashboard developed to analyze customer activity, branch presence, loan performance, NPA/default accounts, and credit card exposure.

## 🎯 Objective
To convert raw banking data into a simple management dashboard highlighting portfolio size, customer activity, and potential risk areas.

## 📊 Key Metrics
- Total Customers: 1,050
- Active Customers: 866
- Total Branches: 30
- Loans Disbursed: ₹1.147M
- Total Loans Outstanding: ₹364M
- NPA + Default Loans: 92
- Total Credit Cards: 500
- Card Outstanding: ₹94M

## 🔍 Key Observations
- A large share of customers are active, indicating relatively strong customer engagement.
- Loan disbursement is significantly higher than the current outstanding portfolio.
- 92 NPA/default loans require attention from a credit-risk perspective.
- Credit card outstanding provides an additional view of customer credit exposure.

## ⚠️ Issues / Judgement
- NPA/default accounts should be monitored closely to reduce potential credit losses.
- Loan disbursement vs. outstanding values should be reviewed to understand repayment and portfolio movement.
- Customer activity can be further segmented by branch and product to identify underperforming areas.
- The dashboard provides descriptive insights; deeper risk analysis would require transaction-level and repayment-history data.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts
- Data Cleaning
- Dashboarding
- KPI Analysis

## 📁 Project Outcome
Created a concise management dashboard to support quick review of customer activity, lending performance, and credit exposure.
